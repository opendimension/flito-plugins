# Account summary

1. If you only have a name, find the company with `search`. If more than one matches, ask which one.
2. Use `get_account_summary` for it.
3. Tell the user:
   - what the company is and how big it is
   - where things stand: open and won deals, quotes, invoices owed
   - the key people
   - what happened recently, and the open tasks
4. End with the two or three things to do next on this account, with links.

Before changing anything, follow the rules in the working-in-flito skill.
